About FoodWise AI

An AI-powered smart college mess management system designed to predict food demand and reduce food wastage.

🍽️ Project Overview

Making college mess management smarter with Artificial Intelligence.

FoodWise AI is a Smart College Mess Management System that uses Machine Learning to predict the number of food servings required for a particular meal.

The system analyzes factors such as expected student attendance, day, meal type, menu, holidays, special events, semester status, and previous food demand.

Using these factors, FoodWise AI helps college mess administrators prepare an appropriate amount of food and reduce unnecessary food wastage.

🚀 Key Features

Important features available in FoodWise AI.

🤖 AI Demand Prediction

Predict the number of food servings required using a trained Machine Learning model.

📊 Analytics Dashboard

Visualize food demand, student attendance, and food waste trends using interactive charts.

♻️ Waste Analysis

Calculate food wastage and receive recommendations to improve food preparation.

📋 Prediction History

Store and manage previous food demand predictions for future analysis.

💡 Smart Recommendations

Receive AI-powered suggestions based on food demand and food waste data.

📈 Model Performance

View Machine Learning performance metrics such as MAE, MSE, RMSE, and R² Score.

⚙️ How FoodWise AI Works

A simple process for smart food planning.

1️⃣ Enter Data

Enter meal information, expected student attendance, menu details, holidays, and previous demand.

2️⃣ AI Prediction

The Machine Learning model analyzes the provided information and predicts food demand.

3️⃣ Smart Decision

The mess administrator receives a recommended food preparation quantity with a safety buffer.

🎯 Our Goal

Building smarter and more sustainable college mess management.

The main goal of FoodWise AI is to reduce food wastage while ensuring that enough food is prepared for students. By combining Artificial Intelligence, historical data, and food waste analysis, the system supports smarter and more sustainable decision-making in college mess management.
